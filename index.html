Leadership

Ryan Milliman

CEO & Founder · Investment Banker

When you work with Pythagorean Group, you work directly with Ryan — every mandate, every conversation, every decision. No hand-offs to junior staff, no layers between you and the person actually doing the work.

Ryan Milliman is the CEO of Pythagorean Group and a licensed investment banker with GT Securities, where he focuses on strategic capital formation, sponsor advisory, private credit, growth equity, and complex private market transactions.

With more than 20 years in financial services, Ryan has raised and advised on millions of dollars across private equity, venture capital, infrastructure, real estate, healthcare, industrial, and special situation transactions. He has built trusted relationships with family offices, UHNW individuals, private equity groups, lenders, and institutional investors.

Ryan previously served as Supervisory Principal for Alternative Investments at one of the largest independent broker-dealers in the country, overseeing complex alternative investment offerings and regulatory compliance.

Ryan earned his B.S. in Business Administration from Cal Poly, San Luis Obispo, with a minor in International Trade and Development.
